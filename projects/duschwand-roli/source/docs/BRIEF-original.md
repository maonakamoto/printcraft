# Duschwand Roli — Project Brief

## Overview
Custom shower wall mural for Roli's houseboat bathroom (River Queen, K 2070).
A gift featuring Roli and friends in a cartoon/illustration style with **recognizable faces**.

## Dimensions
Two panels forming an L-corner:
- **Left panel:** 80 × 200 cm
- **Right panel:** 120 × 200 cm (main/more visible)
- Total unwrapped: 200 × 200 cm

### Print Resolution (150 DPI)
- Left: 4,724 × 11,811 px
- Right: 7,087 × 11,811 px
- Full: 11,811 × 11,811 px

## Scene
Water/lake setting. The blue **Amphicar** (license plate B-AP 670) is center stage on the 120 cm panel.

## Style Options
1. **Cartoon/illustration** — Vibrant, fun, recognizable faces (current direction)
2. **Hyper-realistic** — Photo-real but stylized
3. **Both** — Cartoon with photo-realistic faces composited in

## Constraints
- No local GPU — all generation via cloud APIs
- Faces must be recognizable (ChatGPT/DALL-E can't do this — EU restrictions)
- Must survive printing at 192×200 cm without visible artifacts
- Waterproof print material (handled by print shop)
